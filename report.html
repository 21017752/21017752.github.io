📑 政府工作报告 中英双语素材库

使用说明

政府工作报告是 CATTI 二笔、二口考试 最高频、最核心、最标准 的政经翻译素材，句式严谨、术语官方、适配考场评分标准，适合每日精读、积累固定表达、仿写训练。

双语精读段落（精选备考版）

段落 1：经济发展与民生保障

中文：

过去一年，我国经济发展遇到疫情等国内外多重超预期因素冲击。在党中央坚强领导下，我们高效统筹疫情防控和经济社会发展，根据病毒变化和防控形势，优化调整疫情防控措施。面对经济新的下行压力，果断应对、及时调控，动用近年储备的政策工具，靠前实施既定政策举措，坚定不移推进供给侧结构性改革，出台实施稳经济一揽子政策和接续措施，部署稳住经济大盘工作，着力保市场主体保就业保民生，推动经济回归正常轨道。

English：

Over the past year, China's economy has grown remarkably, surging ahead against headwinds. The economy registered steady and sound growth. Gross domestic product (GDP) grew by 5 percent year-on-year, reaching 149.9 trillion yuan. Overall employment remained stable, with 12.44 million urban jobs created. Foreign trade registered rapid growth, and substantial advances were made in export diversification, and a basic equilibrium was maintained in the balance of payments.

段落 2：科技创新与产业升级

中文：

一年来，我国科技创新成果丰硕。全社会研发经费投入强度提升到2.8%，我国在全球创新指数中的排名从第十一位上升到第十位。新能源汽车产销量超过900万辆，人工智能、大数据、云计算等新技术新业态新模式蓬勃发展，产业结构持续优化。高技术制造业增加值增长5.9%，装备制造业增加值增长6.5%，消费品工业增加值增长6.1%。

English：

Over the past year, China has achieved dynamic, innovation-driven, and high-quality development. New quality productive forces grew steadily, and numerous new advances were made in science and technology. Great headway was made in the research, development, and application of artificial intelligence (AI), homebred robots, and quantum technology. New breakthroughs were made in the independent research and development (R&D) of chips. The Tiangong-2 space lab embarked on an inspirational exploration mission. Large-scale application of the BeiDou Navigation Satellite System was extended across various sectors; construction began on the hydropower project in the upper reaches of the Yarlung Zangbo River; the Fujian, China's first domestically built full-scale carrier equipped with an electromagnetic catapult system, was officially commissioned; and China-made AI models spurred the development of the global open-source AI ecosystem.

段落 3：绿色发展与生态保护

中文：

生态环境保护和绿色发展取得新进展。单位国内生产总值能耗下降5.8%，生态环境质量持续改善。

English：

The industrial structure continued to improve. The value added of high-tech manufacturing and equipment manufacturing grew by 5.9 percent and 2 percent respectively, while that of output of industrial robots and integrated circuits rose by 26 percent and 9 percent respectively. New-energy vehicle output exceeded 16 million units, and the nation's first domestically built full-scale carrier equipped with an electromagnetic catapult system, was officially commissioned; and China-made AI models spurred the development of the global open-source AI ecosystem. A drop of 5.1 percent in energy consumption per unit of GDP contributed to continued improvement in the environment.

📝 翻译句式模板库（CATTI笔译/汉译英直接套用）

1. 关于“坚持/贯彻/落实”

中文：我们将坚持稳中求进工作总基调，完整、准确、全面贯彻新发展理念。

模板：We will continue to pursue the general principle of making progress while maintaining stability, and fully and faithfully apply the new development philosophy.

2. 关于“推进/推动”

中文：我们将稳步推进高水平对外开放，持续优化营商环境。

模板：We will steadily advance high-standard opening-up and continue to improve the business environment.

3. 关于“加强/强化”

中文：要加强重点领域风险防范，守住不发生系统性风险的底线。

模板：We will strengthen risk prevention in key areas and keep the bottom line of preventing systemic risks.

💡 备考与精读指南（如何高效利用这份素材）

  • 每日精读：每天选1段，先自己英译汉/汉译英，再对照官方译文找差距。
  • 跟读训练：对着英文译文大声朗读，熟悉政经文本的节奏和停顿，适配口译考试。
  • 术语积累：把陌生表达记在Anki里，考前反复刷，保证术语不写错。
  • 仿写练习：套用句式模板，自己造1-2句同主题的句子，加深记忆。
